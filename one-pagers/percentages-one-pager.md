# Feature One-Pager: Percentages Quiz App

**Author**: [TBD] | **Date**: 2026-02-13 | **Status**: Draft

---

## The Problem (What & Why)
Existing quiz apps are dominated by trivia, leaving a gap for a game that challenges players with logic and "outside-the-box" thinking. This matters because there is a growing audience of users who seek intellectually stimulating entertainment and enjoy puzzle-solving in both solo and social settings.

**Impact**: This affects puzzle enthusiasts and social gamers who are looking for a fresh and engaging quiz experience. This is a daily or weekly activity for many.

---

## The Solution (How)
We are building a mobile and web quiz app called "Percentages," inspired by "The 1% Club." The app will feature a unique, AI-powered question generation system to ensure content is always fresh and challenging.

**Key Capabilities**:
- Playable in both solo and multiplayer (up to 100 players) modes.
- AI-driven question generation for endless replayability.
- Host-controlled game sessions for a live, interactive experience.

---

## Success Looks Like
**Primary Metric**: Achieve 1,000 Monthly Active Users (MAU) within 3 months of launch.

**User Feedback**: We expect to hear that users find the questions "clever" and "challenging," and that the multiplayer mode is a "fun activity for groups."

---

## Trade-offs & Scope
**In Scope**:
- Solo and multiplayer game modes.
- Core UI for gameplay.
- AI question generation (Phase 2).
- Question database with API.

**Out of Scope**:
- User profiles and leaderboards (initially).
- In-app purchases or monetization features.
- Different game modes beyond the core "Percentages" format.

**Key Trade-off**: We are prioritizing the core gameplay experience and unique question-generation system over broader social features like user profiles for the initial launch. This allows us to validate the core concept first.

## Risks
1. **AI Question Quality**: The AI may not consistently generate high-quality, clever puzzles. **Mitigation**: Implement a human review and curation process for AI-generated questions initially.
2. **Player Drop-off**: The questions might be too difficult, leading to high player churn. **Mitigation**: Carefully balance the difficulty curve and provide a smooth onboarding experience.
