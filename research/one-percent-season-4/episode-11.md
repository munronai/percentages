## 80% Question

**Question:** Rebecca and Thomas are going to the zoo. They want to see lions, tigers and bears but they are in three separate areas. Bears are not found in the Red Zone. The Green and Yellow Zones have no lions. Tigers occupy the Green Zone. Where do Rebecca and Thomas go to see the lions?
* (A) Red Zone
* (B) Green Zone
* (C) Yellow Zone

**Answer:** A: Red Zone. [The question says the Green and Yellow Zones have no lions so they can only be in the Red Zone.]

## 60% Question

**Question:** Which capital letter below is the only one described by the word in which it features?
* (A) penultimatE
* (B) cenTral
* (C) iNiital

**Answer:** B: cenTral. E is not the second to letter in penultimate. N is not the first letter in initial. T IS the middle letter in central.

## 50% Question

**Image:** `[Image Credit: ITV]`

**Question:** What is the smallest number of eggs that need to be moved so that no two eggs are sitting directly next to each other vertically or horizontally?

**Answer:** 2. [You can move the egg in the first row, fourth column to the top-left corner. Then move the egg in the bottom-left corner up one.]

## 45% Question

**Question:** In this puzzle you need to stay IN the game. Which of these words is the odd one out?
* (A) JURY
* (B) CREASE
* (C) DEED
* (D) CRY

**Answer:** D: CRY. If you put IN before the other words, it makes new words [INJURY, INCREASE, INDEED]. INCRY is not a word although OUTCRY is.

## 40% Question

**Question:** Answer this question: How many Ps are there in total in the following tongue twister?
> PAUL AND PEARL POPPER PACKED POTS OF PEAS. IN EACH POT THERE WERE 100 PEAS. PAUL PACKED 16 POTS AND PEARL PACKED 19 POTS. WHEN PAUL AND PEARL PACKED ALL THEIR POTS, THERE WERE SO MANY PEAS!

**Answer:** 21. The question is asking you to count the number of times the letter P appears, not the food item.

## 30% Question

**Image:** `[Image Credit: ITV]`

**Question:** Using only the faces of the dice you can see and each of them just once, how many different ways can you add them together to make a total of ten?

**Answer:** 3. The only combinations are 5+4+1, 5+3+2, and 4+3+2+1.

## 25% Question

**Question:** Which of these statements is true?
* (A) In this statement, the word “the” occurs twice and so does the word “in”
* (B) In this statement, the word “and” occurs twice and so does the word “word”
* (C) In this statement, the word “in” occurs twice and so does the word “so”

**Answer:** C. [The words in quotations still count.]
