# The 1% Club - UK Season 4, Episode 5

## 90% Question

**Question:** Which of these is the largest?
* (A) CIRCLE
* (B) TRIANGLE
* (C) RECTANGLE
* (D) SQUARE

**Answer:** C: RECTANGLE. RECTANGLE is nine letters long. All the others are shorter. [Yes, the wording of the question is strange.]

## 60% Question

**Question:** What four-letter word replaces the question mark?
> SOFTENING –> SONG
> RADIOACTIVE –> RAVE
> WHITECOAT –> WHAT
> FAVOURITE –> ?

**Answer:** FATE. Each new word is created by using the first two and the last two letters of the original.

---

## 50% Question

**Question:** Adding what single letter to CONTACT makes it smaller?

**Answer:** R. Adding an R makes it CONTRACT.

---

## 45% Question

**Question:** If you remove a playing card from the first word, a pet from the second word, an insect from the third word and a drink from the fourth word, what number appears?
> FACE COAT AUNT TEAR

**Answer:** FOUR. [Remove ACE from FACE, CAT from COAT, ANT from AUNT, and TEA from TEAR.]

---

## 40% Question

**Question:** What three-letter word has a similar meaning when read backwards and also if you move the first letter to the end becomes apt?

**Answer:** TAP. When read backwards, TAP becomes PAT and if you move the first letter to the end, TAP becomes APT.

## 25% Question

**Question:** Which three different whole numbers – all lower than 10 – give you the same total whether you add them together or multiply them together?

**Answer:** 1, 2, and 3. 1 + 2 + 3 = 6 and 1 x 2 x 3 = 6.

## 15% Question

**Question:** If my old brown woolly jumper contains two holes for every o in this sentence, how many holes are there?

**Answer:** 22. The letter o appears 11 times. 11 x 2 = 22.

## 10% Question

**Image:** `[Image Credit: iTV]`

**Question:** There are four letters written on the circle. The other letters are all outside the circle. You need to turn the circle clockwise until a colour is spelt out. How many 90 degree turns do you need to make?

**Answer:** 2. After two turns, the p turns to d so the word red appears.

## 5% Question

**Question:** Today, the three witches in Macbeth are discussing when the three should meet again. Witch 1 is available every fourth day, Witch 2 is available every fifth day, and Witch 3 is available every sixth day. How many days from today will they be able to meet again?

**Answer:** 60 days. 60 is the first number that is divisible by four, five and six.

**Answer:** FLORIST. Where he could, he replaced a letter with a digit that starts with that letter.
