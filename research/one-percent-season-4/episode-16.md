## 80% Question

**Question:** If holes are goals, who would win between these two teams?
* A) Arsenal
* B) Liverpool

**Answer:** B. [Liverpool has four holes, the holes in ‘e’, ‘p’, and the two ‘o’ letters. Arsenal has three holes.]

## 70% Question

**Question:** Which of these is an anagram of LIONEL MESSI?
* A) LONE MISSILE
* B) LION IN SLIME
* C) NO SMILES LEE

**Answer:** A.

> BICYCLE _ _ _ _

**Question:** Which of these displays the letters of GOALKEEPER in alphabetical order?

* B) A PAIR OF SOCKS
* C) A PAIR OF GLOVES

## 25% Question

**Question:** As well as being good at football, Harry Kane loves calligraphy. When he writes out his name exactly as it appears below, what is the only letter he can write without going back over a line or taking his pen off the page?
> H A R R Y K A N E

**Answer:** N. [N is the only letter there that can be written with one continuous line without going back over a line.]

---

## 20% Question

**Question:** England are playing Germany in the World Cup final. Just after Germany score their first goal from a penalty, the whistle blows for half-time and England end the first half three goals ahead. In the second half, Germany draw level before England score two late goals to win the game. In total, how many goals were scored in the game?

**Answer:** 10. [The final score is Germany 4 – England 6.]

## 15% Question

**Image:** `[Image Credit: ITV]`

**Question:** If the size of your feet and the size of your balls don’t matter, what is the maximum number of pairs of one foot and one ball that you can make from the image below?

**Answer:** 16. [The phrase “pairs of one foot and one ball” is meant to trip you up, but there are 16 balls and 18 feet so the max number of pairs is 16.]

## 10% Question

**Question:** Of the twenty-six letters of the alphabet, how many different letters are used in the names on these four football shirts?

**Answer:** 15. [The letters are R, O, N, E, Y, G, A, D, B, C, J, H, N, K, I.]

## 5% Question

**Image:** `[Image Credit: ITV]`

**Question:** Thierry passes to Cristiano, Christiano passes to Johan, Johan passes to Stanley, Stanley passes to Zinedine, Zinedine passes to Cristiano, Christiano passes to Erling, Erling passes to Zinedine. How many full triangles have been made?

**Answer:** 1. Only the passes between Zinedine, Cristiano, and Erling make a triangle.

## 1% Question

**Image:** `[Image Credit: ITV]`

**Question:** In the opening verse to the original version of ‘Three Lions’, what TWO words feature exactly three times in the lyrics?

**Answer:** “Know” and “it”. [Not exactly the hardest 1% question we’ve seen, but it’s for charity so we’re not surprised this is easier.]
