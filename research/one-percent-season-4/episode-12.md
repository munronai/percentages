## 50% Question

**Question:** If a consonant is worth one point and a vowel is worth two points, which word in this sentence scores the most points?

**Answer:** CONSONANT. The word has 6 consonants and 3 vowels which equals 12 points – more than any other word in the sentence.

## 45% Question

**Question:** Clare opens up an envelope posted through her letterbox and inside she sees this code written in the card. What do the Hs stand for?

**Answer:** Happy. [The letters represent the first letter in the traditional “Happy Birthday To You” song.]

## 40% Question

**Image:** `[Image Credit: ITV]`

**Question:** Lisa wants her bathroom floor to have more suns than moons, more moons than hearts and more hearts than diamonds. Which tile can she use to complete her design?
* (A), (B), or (C)

**Answer:** A. Option B gives you more diamonds than hearts. Option C gives you the same number of suns and moons.

---

## 35% Question

**Image:** `[Image Credit: ITV]`

**Question:** Jessica wants to paint her family’s nails but only wants to paint every THIRD nail. She starts counting from the little finger on Charlie’s left hand. Who is the only person who will NOT have either of their thumbs painted?
* (A) Charlie
* (B) Joanne
* (C) Marcus
* (D) Chris

**Answer:** C: Marcus. Jessica will paint the right thumbs of Charlie and Chris and the left thumb of Joanne. [Also, Marcus’s thumbs would be in position 25 and 26, two numbers not divisible by 3.]

---

## 30% Question

**Question:** Without reordering any letters, which six-letter food appears in this sentence?
> DAISY THE COW PRODUCED EXCELLENT PRODUCTS BUT TERRIFIED VISITORS TO THE FARM

**Answer:** BUTTER. [From BUT and TERRIFIED.]

## 20% Question

**Question:** Changing the first letter of each of these words will create new words with a theme in common. What is the common theme?
> DOG, PAIN, FIST, MAIL

**Answer:** Weather. [DOG can become FOG, PAIN to RAIN, FIST to MIST, and MAIL to HAIL.]

---

## 15% Question

**Question:** Which two options need to be swapped to make this list correct?
> A – CORN
> B – LEAF
> C – MAIL
> D – RAIL
> E – SAW

**Answer:** C and E. All the words can have a letter added to the front to make a new word. The added letter is one further along in the alphabet each time. [ACORN, BLEAF, EMAIL, FRAIL, GSAW]

## 1% Question

**Image:** `[Image Credit: ITV]`

**Question:** If you remove each pair of rhyming words and rearrange the red letters above the remaining words, what new common four-letter word can you make?

**Answer:** HEAD. [Remove all the rhyming pairs will get you HUMPTY, HOB, TEENSY, and WALKIE for the letters A, D, E, and H.]
