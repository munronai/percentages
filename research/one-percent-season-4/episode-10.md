# The 1% Club - UK Season 4, Episode 10

## 90% Question

**Question:**  Which of these options completes the bottom picture?
* (A) or (B)

**Answer:** B. In the bottom picture, you can see that the person has their glasses hanging on their shirt so they can’t have them on their face at the same time.

---

## 80% Question

**Question:** Which of these is the odd one out when there is more than one?
* (A), (B), (C), or (D)

**Answer:** A. B, C, and D have plurals that end in ‘s’ – hats, cars and chairs. The plural of mouse is mice.

---

## 70% Question

**Question:** What four-letter word logically fills the gap?
> Characters in books who start off EVIL and VILE usually draw a VEIL over their behaviour and decide they want to ____ a purposeful life.

**Answer:** LIVE. All the words in uppercase are anagrams of each other. LIVE is the only other word that uses the same letters and fits the gap.

---

## 60% Question

**Question:** Asher’s local set of traffic lights has the following sequence on repeat. Red for nine seconds, red and amber for two seconds, green for ten seconds and amber for three seconds. Which colour light is on for the longest amount of time within the sequence?
* (A) Red
* (B) Amber
* (C) Green

**Answer:** A: Red. In every sequence, red is on for 11 seconds, amber is only on for 5 and green for 10.

---

## 50% Question

**Question:** The nine-letter name of which insect is written below with its letters in alphabetical order?

**Answer:** BUMBLEBEE

## 45% Question

**Question:** How many different letters appear more than once in the words ‘ONE PERCENT CLUB’?

**Answer:** 3. [There are two N letters, three E letters, and two C letters.]

---

## 40% Question

**Question:** Here are some very clever chemical elements. What letter replaces the question mark?

**Answer:** S. The chemical symbols spell out the word ‘GENIUS’.

---

## 35% Question

**Question:** Five siblings’ birthdays all fall in the same month. Bob’s is six days after Anna’s. Cat’s is seven days after Bob’s Dan’s is eight days after Cat’s. And Eric’s is nine days after Dan’s. In which month were they all born?
* (A) February,
* (B) April
* (C) June
* (D) August

**Answer:** D: August. The month needs to be 31 days long and August is the only option with 31 days.

---

## 30% Question

**Question:** What two well-known eight-letter words are made when the following are correctly paired up?

**Answer:** NOTEBOOK and WINDMILL.

---

## 25% Question

**Question:** There is a nine-letter word. When you remove the first five letters some must remain and when you remove the last four letters the word becomes whole. What is the nine-letter word?

**Answer:** WHOLESOME. Without the five first letters, you have SOME. Without the last four letters, the word becomes WHOLE.

---

## 20% Question

**Question:** What will be the next year where the four digits will add up to the same total as the digits in 2025?

**Answer:** 2034. 2 + 0 + 2 + 5 = 9. 2 + 0 + 3 + 4 = 9.

---

## 15% Question

**Question:** What house number and street is represented below?

**Answer:** 10 Downing Street. ING appears down 10 times = 10 Downing Street.

---

## 10% Question

**Question:** What number replaces the question mark in the picture below?

**Answer:** 12. The number in the roof is revealed by adding the numbers in the two windows together and taking away the number on the door. 5 + 12 = 17 and 17 – 5 = 12.

---

## 5% Question

**Question:** What is the hidden pattern in these four phrases that makes them have something in common?

**Answer:** COMPASS DIRECTIONS. A compass point appears in each pair of words. [NOR and TH in the first pair make NORTH, EA and ST in the second pair make EAST, S and OUTH in the third pair make SOUTH, and WE and ST in the fourth pair make WEST.]

---

## 1% Question

**Question:** If you remove all the underlined letters from the sentence below, what word can be spelt if you rearrange all of the remaining letters?
> ONE SELLING FRESH SALMON

**Answer:** FISHMONGER. [The full sentence provides a hint to the word they’re looking for.]
