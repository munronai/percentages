## 60% Question

**Question:** Who does not belong in this group?
* A) CARL NOONAN
* B) DAN THOMAS
* C) MEG NIGHTINGALE
* D) BECKY MORNINGSIDE

**Answer:** B. All the others have a time of day in their name. [NOON in NOONAN, NIGHT in NIGHTINGALE, MORNING in MORNINGSIDE.]

## 40% Question

**Question:** 5 May 2025 is known as square root day. The previous square root day was in 2016. In which month did it occur?

**Answer:** April. Square root day occurs when the date and the month number are identical and multiply together to make the last two digits of the year. 5 x 5 = 25 and 4 x 4 = 16.

## 1% Question

**Image:** `[Image Credit: ITV]`

**Question:** What number sequence replaces the question mark?

**Answer:** 12345. The numbers indicate the order the letters come alphabetically. The letters in FORTY are in alphabetical order so the answer is 12345.
