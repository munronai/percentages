## 10% Question

**Question:** What job do you get by following these dates?
> 1st FEBRUARY
> 2nd MARCH
> 3rd APRIL
> 1st MAY
> 4th JUNE
> 9th SEPTEMBER

**Answer:** FARMER. The date corresponds to the position of the letter in the name of the month so 1st FEBRUARY is F, 2nd MARCH is A and so on.

## 5% Question

**Image:** `[Image Credit: ITV]`

**Question:** Look at this shape. The top two rows contain four triangles of the same size. Including the top two rows, how many of those triangles are needed in total to fill this shape?

**Answer:** 25. Each row increases by two triangles so the next rows will have 5, 7, and 9 triangles. 1 + 3 + 5 + 7 + 9 = 25.
