## 45% Question

**Question:** Which of these words can have two of its letters swapped over and still result in a valid word?
* (A) CONVERSATION
* (B) DIALOGUE
* (C) CHAT

**Answer:** A. [The V and S in CONVERSATION can be swapped to form the word CONSERVATION.]

## 20% Question

**Question:** These two leaves in Kate’s garden always attract ladybirds. The left leaf always has ladybirds with four spots on and the right leaf always has ladybirds with six spots on. How many spots would there be in total if one more ladybird lands on each leaf?

**Answer:** 44. The left leaf has five ladybirds for 20 spots. The right leaf has four landbirds for 24 spots. This results in a total of 44 dots.

## 15% Question

**Image:** `[Image Credit: ITV]`

**Question:** If you remove the word ‘BRA’ from each of these lines of letters, how many lines will end with a vowel?

**Answer:** 5. Because every BRA is preceded by an A, exactly the same number of lines will end with a vowel.

## 10% Question

**Image:** `[Image Credit: ITV]`

**Question:** In this puzzle, which one of these words doesn’t belong?

**Answer:** HUNG. All the other wards can be paired to make the names of countries (GERMANY, DENMARK, NORWAY, ICELAND, and SPAIN). You would need ARY to make HUNG+ARY.

## 5% Question

**Question:** How many different words are there in the tongue twister below?
> SHE SELLS SEA SHELLS BY THE SEASHORE. THE SHELLS SHE SELLS ARE SURELY SEA SHELLS. SO IF SHE SELLS SHELLS ON THE SEASHORE, I AM SURE SHE SELLS SEASHORE SHELLS.

**Answer:** 15. [The words are SHE, SELLS, SEA, SHELLS, BY, THE, SEASHORE, ARE, SURELY, SO, IF, ON, I, AM, and SURE.]
