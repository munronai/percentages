# Quantitative Data: Puzzle vs. Trivia Game Market

**Date**: 18 February 2026

This document summarizes quantitative data comparing the market performance and user engagement of the Puzzle and Trivia mobile game genres, intended to provide supporting evidence for the "Percentages" PRD.

---

## 1. Executive Summary

The data overwhelmingly shows that the **Puzzle genre is significantly larger, more lucrative, and commands higher user loyalty than the Trivia genre**. Market reports from 2023 consistently place Puzzle as a top-tier category in mobile gaming, while Trivia is often treated as a small sub-genre of Puzzle. This strongly supports the hypothesis that there is a large, established market for puzzle-based mobile games.

---

## 2. Market Size and Revenue

Puzzle games represent one of the largest segments of the mobile gaming market.

- **Global Revenue**: Puzzle was the **second-biggest revenue-generating genre** on mobile in 2023, surpassed only by RPGs. The genre's revenue grew by 14.4% in 2023. [1]
- **Market Value**: Over an 11-year period leading up to June 2023, mobile puzzle games generated **$42 billion** in global revenue. [2]
- **Downloads**: The Puzzle genre saw over **1.2 billion downloads** globally in 2023, a 6.1% increase for the year. [3]
- **Trivia as a Sub-Genre**: In some analyses, Trivia is categorized as a sub-genre within the broader Puzzle market, accounting for **6% of the Puzzle market's total installs**. [2]

**Conclusion**: The Puzzle genre has a proven, multi-billion dollar market with high user demand. The Trivia market is substantially smaller.

---

## 3. User Engagement and Retention

Puzzle games demonstrate exceptionally high user engagement and loyalty metrics.

- **Player Loyalty**: A 2024 report analyzing user behavior referred to the Puzzle genre as the **"champion of player loyalty,"** achieving a loyalty rating of 85, significantly higher than RPGs (75) and Strategy games (71). [4]
- **Retention Rates**: The genre shows strong user retention, with average Day 1 retention at **31.85%** and Day 30 retention around **6%**. [5]
- **Monetization Speed**: Puzzle games convert users to paying players quickly, with an average "time to first purchase" of just **1.6 to 1.7 days** after installation. [6]
- **Trivia Engagement**: While specific trivia titles like "QuizDuel!" and "Kahoot!" show healthy weekly active user counts (in the hundreds of thousands in Europe), their engagement can be hampered by factors like intrusive advertisements. [7]

**Conclusion**: The Puzzle genre has a demonstrated ability to retain users long-term and convert them to paying customers effectively. This indicates a strong appetite for engaging puzzle content.
