## Product Context
The goal of this project is to develop "Percentages," a multi-user quiz application styled after the UK TV show "The 1% Club." The application will test players' lateral thinking and problem-solving skills with questions graded by the estimated percentage of the population capable of answering them. It must support both large group play (up to 100 participants) and single-player modes.

### Product Name
Percentages

### Product Vision
Provide friends and family with an online gameshow experience either as a single player or part of a multiplayer experience.

### Target Users
- **Primary Persona**: GameHost - The real enthusiast for the lateral thinking puzzles. Loves to play alongside TV quizzes. Able to setup a game session, configure settings as necessary, invite other players as necessary and start the game. Needs to be able to control the flow of the game and assist other players in resolving technical difficulties.
- **Secondary Persona**: Player - Generally, loves to take part in quizzes but may not be familiar with the lateral thinking style. Needs to be able to find open sessions created by GameHost, including direct invitations. Must find the game style and interactions intuitive.
- **Other Stakeholders**: Viewers - Loves to play alongside (or view) a quiz or gameshow without taking part directly. Needs to be able to find game sessions to watch, to see the questions and record answers for their own amusement, and to see the overall state of the game (how many players answered correctly, which players remain in the game).

### Core Value Proposition
Competitive multiplayer experience for lateral thinking quiz where question difficulty increases according to percentage of UK population that can correctly answer.

### Key Metrics
- **North Star Metric**: 
97% success rate that Player and GameHost rate the question as meeting the North Star Criteria.
- **North Star Criteria**:
Generation of lateral thinking questions on-the-fly with verification that the question has both an answer and verification that the question could be answered by a particular percentage of the UK adult population. 
- **Supporting Metrics**: 
100% of games with less than 10 players complete successfully for all players.
90% of games running simultaneously with more than 10 players complete successfully.
Successful completion is where player drop-off not caused by in-game technical issue.

## Common Tasks

### When Writing User Stories
- Follow the format: "As a [user], I want [goal], so that [benefit]"
- Include acceptance criteria
- Add context about user pain points
- Reference relevant data or feedback
- Estimate story points collaboratively with engineering

### When Analyzing Features
- Consider user value, business value, and technical feasibility
- Identify dependencies and risks
- Define clear success metrics
- Map to strategic goals
- Consider MVP vs. full solution

### When Reviewing Feedback

- Distinguish between stated wants and underlying needs
